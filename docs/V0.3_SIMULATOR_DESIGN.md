## 1. Executive Summary & Objective

The **Strider32 v0.3 3D Robot Simulator** is a complete, browser-native 3D visualization and simulation suite for the Strider32 quadruped platform. It allows users to simulate, test, and manipulate 8-DOF Kinematics, Cartesian foot trajectories, stance/swing gait phases, keyframe animations, and browser Gamepad controls in real time—100% offline without physical robot hardware or external CDN dependencies.
